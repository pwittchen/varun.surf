Privacy Policy

This policy covers the VARUN.SURF website (varun.surf) and the VARUN.SURF Android app. Both are run by Piotr Wittchen, who is the data controller. Last updated: 25 October 2026.

In short

  • There are no accounts, no ads and no data sold or shared for advertising.
  • The Android app has no analytics, tracking or crash reporting SDKs.
  • Your location never leaves your device.
  • Favourites, alerts and settings are stored on your device, not on our server.

Android app

  • Location (approximate, only if you allow it) is used on the device to sort spots by distance. It is never sent to our server or anyone else.
  • Favourites, wind alerts, settings and the calculator inputs are stored on the device. Wind alerts are checked on the device against the public forecast.
  • Android backup: if backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android copies favourites, alerts and settings to your Google account, under Google's privacy policy. The cached forecasts and the session cookie are excluded.
  • Notifications are only shown for the wind alerts you create.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all of this data.

Requests to varun.surf

  • The server hands out a session cookie that is needed to use the API. It is a signed expiry time valid for 24 hours: it does not identify you and is not linked to any profile.
  • The Android app sends an X-Client header with its version number, so we can keep the API compatible with older app versions.
  • Like every web server, ours sees your IP address. It is used for rate limiting and protection against abuse, and appears in the web server's access logs, which are kept only for a limited time for security purposes. The application's own logs record the requested path and the browser or app name (user agent), not the IP address, and are held in memory only.
  • Traffic passes through Cloudflare, which provides caching and protection against attacks.

Website

  • Your favourites, spot order, theme and language are kept in your browser's local storage and never leave it.
  • The website uses DataFast to count visits and see which pages are read. It may set a first-party cookie to tell unique visitors apart, and is not used for advertising. The Android app does not use it.

Third-party services

Some content is loaded straight from other services, which then receive your IP address and the request (for maps, the area being viewed):

  • map tiles: Esri World Imagery, OpenFreeMap (app) and OpenStreetMap (website)
  • map and globe libraries on the website: unpkg

Links to Windguru, Windfinder, webcams, weather stations and other sites open those sites, where their own privacy policies apply.

AI analysis

The AI forecast analysis and the ICM forecast reading are generated by OpenAI when someone presses the button on a spot page. Only the spot's forecast, its description and the ICM meteogram image are sent to OpenAI - nothing about you. The result is shared with every visitor of that spot for 24 hours.

If an analysis is wrong or offensive, use the report option under it or write to us.

Children, your rights and changes

  • The service is not directed at children under 13.
  • Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, or deletion of, any personal data we hold about you (in practice, IP addresses in the server logs) and complain to your data protection authority (in Poland, the UODO).
  • Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
